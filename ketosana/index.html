Ketosana shopping checklist

  • Check the label and serving size. Compare the ingredients and quantities on the seller’s current label before choosing a bundle.
  • Compare the total cost per bottle. Divide the full checkout total, including shipping and taxes, by the number of bottles.
  • Check delivery and returns. Confirm shipping to your address and check the return deadline, eligibility for opened bottles and who pays return postage.
